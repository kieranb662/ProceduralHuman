Dress Up / Clothing Slots

The default is an unclothed, smooth doll base with bare feet and five toes per foot. Clothing is removable and follows the body rig; it never replaces body geometry.

All clothing slots empty.

Outfit API

window.proceduralHuman.character.setClothing(slot, item) swaps one slot. setOutfit({ torso, arms, legs, feet }) swaps a complete outfit; omitted slots are cleared. Use "none" to undress. clothingSlots exposes each slot's items and current mesh. Slots cover both limbs and share the base skeleton and bind pose.

4. Deformable Face & FACS Sliders

The tongue rests behind the lower incisors. Tongue Out opens the jaw for clearance; Tip Bend curls the exposed tip up or down. API: character.faceSliders.tongueOut (0-1), tongueCurl (-1 to 1). TongueBase, TongueMid and TongueTip are jaw-parented animation bones.

Gaze also turns and tilts the head. Use Face Close-Up and orbit to inspect the jaw and neck from the side, back, or below.

Textures & Materials

Changes preview live. Body and head share rest-position pores, anatomical color, and roughness masks. Subsurface scattering is a shadow-aware direct-light wrapped-diffuse/backscatter approximation with estimated thickness, not full diffusion. Skin bump height is in millimeters; base surface properties are independent.

Surface properties

Reusable function

Copy or download createProceduralMaterials(palette = {}). Requires THREE and a browser document. Override colors via palette.skin/shirt/shorts/hair, procedural settings via palette.textures, and slot properties via palette.materials[index]. Skin uses skinTileSize in meters, skinPoreDepth (0-2), skinColorVariation, skinRoughnessVariation, skinScattering (0-1), and skinThicknessScale (0.25-4). Thickness masks assume this prototype's meter-scale neutral anatomy; standard shadow maps can suppress self-shadowed backscatter. Exported skin bumpScale is in meters. Keep the shader hooks when reusing these materials.
